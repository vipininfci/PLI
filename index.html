Circular Provisions & Calculation Rules Reference

Category I, II, III & IV Staff

• FY 2024-25: 2% of Revised Basic Pay.
• Max overall limit: ₹37,800/-.
• 2025-26 Advance: 2/3rd of 2% Basic Pay (Max ₹25,000/-).

Departmental & DPS Labourers

• 15% of min(Basic + IDA, ₹21,000) × Months.
• DPS includes piece-rate earning + ₹50/day IR.
• FY 24-25 Max: ₹37,800/- | Advance Max: ₹25,000/-.

Leave & Ineligibility Clauses

• Pro-rata deduction for leave > 60 days (excl CL).
• Zero PLI for Leave Without Pay (LWP).
• Trainees/MTs/Deputation: Not admissible.

Advance Recovery / Undertaking

• Excess advance paid is adjustable/recoverable.
• 2025-26 advance requires signed Annexure-I Undertaking to refund if applicable.
